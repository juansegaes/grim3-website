About

GRiM3 is a symposium organized by graduate students, for graduate students in mathematics and statistics.

It gives participants the chance to present their research, whether completed work or work in progress, in a supportive setting, receive feedback from peers, and build connections with students from other institutions. Last year's symposium welcomed participants from three different states, and we are hoping to grow that community this year.

The 2027 edition takes place on Feb 20, 2027 at UNC Charlotte, organized by the Math Graduate Student Association.
